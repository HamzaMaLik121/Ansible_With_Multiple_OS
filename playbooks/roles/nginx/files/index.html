03

Secrets handling

Credentials in secret.yml are encrypted at rest, and task output is suppressed so nothing leaks into console logs.

$ ansible-vault encrypt secret.yml
$ ansible-playbook -i host.ini playbooks/show_secret.yml --ask-vault-pass
